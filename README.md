# My Favorite Anime: Bleach

> *"If you can't see me, then you're not looking hard enough."* is not a line from the show, but the spirit of Soul Reapers is all about protecting what you can't always see.

---

## Overview
**Bleach** is a Japanese anime and manga series created by *Tite Kubo*. It follows **Ichigo Kurosaki**, a teenager who gains the powers of a **Soul Reaper** and must protect the living world from evil spirits called **Hollows**.

## Main Characters
1. **Ichigo Kurosaki** – a substitute Soul Reaper
2. **Rukia Kuchiki** – the Soul Reaper who gives Ichigo his powers
3. **Orihime Inoue** – a kind-hearted healer
4. **Uryu Ishida** – a Quincy archer
5. **Yasutora "Chad" Sado** – a powerful, loyal fighter

## Why I Love It
- Epic sword fights and *Bankai* transformations
- Great soundtrack and opening themes
- Memorable villains like *Sosuke Aizen*
- Themes of friendship, courage, and protecting others
- ~~Too many filler episodes~~ The main arcs are amazing
